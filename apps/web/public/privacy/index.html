Privacy

OpenWalk is built so that where you are stays on your phone.

Your location

  • Your phone works out your position, and it is used only on your phone: to draw the blue dot and to plan a route.
  • It is never sent to this site or to anyone else. It is forgotten when you close the map, unless you mark a spot.
  • Marking a spot, such as where you parked, keeps that one point on your phone until you remove it. That happens only when you ask, and the point is never part of a link or a code you share.
  • Searches and routes are worked out on your phone too. What you look for and where you ask to go are not sent anywhere.

What is kept on your phone

  • A copy of the map and the app, so it works without a signal.
  • How you left the map: which layers were switched on and which kinds of place you had hidden.
  • The places you save, the walks from one place to another you save, and the last few places you asked the way to.
  • A note that the “Ready to use offline” message has been shown.
  • Your saved places leave your phone only if you send them yourself, from “My places”, as a code, a link or a file. Those hold the names of the places and nothing else.
  • No cookies, no account, no advertising or tracking identifiers.
  • To remove it all, clear this site’s data in your browser, or delete the app from your home screen.
  • A browser may clear what a site keeps after a week or so without a visit; an iPhone does. Adding the app to your home screen keeps it.

What the server sees

  • Like any website, the server that sends you the map receives ordinary requests for files: your internet address, the time, and which file. The files are the same for everyone and do not show where you are. This server keeps no record of those requests.
  • The map may reach you through a network provider, which handles the same requests on the way.
  • If you open a link someone shared to a particular place, that place is part of the link’s address.
  • There are no analytics, and nothing is loaded from other companies. The app refuses scripts from anywhere but this site.
